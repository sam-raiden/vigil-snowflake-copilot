# POL-AML-003 — Transaction Velocity Monitoring
Version 1.0 | Owner: Financial Crime Compliance | Synthetic policy for the Vigil demo.

## Clause 1.1 — Purpose
This policy addresses accounts used as pass-through channels, where funds arrive and leave almost immediately.

## Clause 2.1 — Velocity Pattern Definition
A velocity pattern exists when a deposit of ₹5,00,000 or more is followed within 24 hours by withdrawals or outgoing transfers totalling 80% or more of the deposit.

## Clause 3.1 — Automatic Hold
On detection, the bank may place an automatic hold on further outgoing transactions on the account pending review.

## Clause 4.1 — Escalation Timeline
A velocity case must be escalated to the AML Compliance Officer within 24 hours of detection.

## Clause 4.2 — Evidentiary Standard
The finding must cite the deposit transaction ID and each outgoing transaction ID, their amounts and timestamps, and the computed percentage moved.
