# POL-CR-001 — NPA Classification & Provisioning
Version 1.0 | Owner: Credit Risk | Synthetic policy for the Vigil demo.

## Clause 1.1 — Purpose
This policy defines when a loan is classified as a Non-Performing Asset (NPA) and the provisioning required, following RBI IRAC norms.

## Clause 2.1 — NPA Threshold
A loan account with more than 90 days past due is classified as an NPA.

## Clause 3.1 — Sub-classification
- Substandard: NPA for up to 12 months.
- Doubtful: NPA for more than 12 months.
- Loss: an asset identified as uncollectible.

## Clause 3.2 — Provisioning
- Substandard: 15% of the outstanding amount.
- Doubtful: 25% to 100% of the outstanding amount, depending on the period in the doubtful category.
- Loss: 100% of the outstanding amount.

## Clause 4.1 — Evidentiary Standard
The finding must cite the loan account ID, days past due, outstanding amount, the classification and the provisioning amount calculated.
