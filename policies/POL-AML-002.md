# POL-AML-002 — High-Risk Jurisdiction Transactions
Version 1.0 | Owner: Financial Crime Compliance | Synthetic policy for the Vigil demo.

## Clause 1.1 — Purpose
This policy governs transactions with counterparties in jurisdictions the FATF identifies as high risk.

## Clause 2.1 — Jurisdiction Definition
High-risk jurisdictions are those on the FATF grey list or black list. For this policy the monitored set includes Myanmar, North Korea, Iran and Panama.

## Clause 3.1 — Enhanced Due Diligence
Any transaction of ₹5,00,000 (₹5L) or more to or from a high-risk jurisdiction requires Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) before or at the time of processing.

## Clause 3.2 — EDD Contents
EDD must record the purpose of the payment, the relationship to the counterparty, and the source of funds.

## Clause 4.1 — Mandatory Escalation
A remittance meeting Clause 3.1 without a completed EDD record must be escalated to the AML Compliance Officer within 24 hours.

## Clause 4.2 — Evidentiary Standard
The finding must cite the transaction ID, amount, counterparty name and country, and whether an EDD record exists.
