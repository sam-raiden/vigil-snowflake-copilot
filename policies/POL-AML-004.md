# POL-AML-004 — STR Filing Timelines & KYC Obligations
Version 1.0 | Owner: Financial Crime Compliance | Synthetic policy for the Vigil demo.

## Clause 1.1 — Purpose
This policy sets the timeline for filing a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR) and the KYC conditions that affect it.

## Clause 2.1 — STR Filing Timeline
An STR must be filed with FIU-IND within 7 working days of the AML Compliance Officer concluding that a transaction is suspicious, consistent with PMLA Rule 8.

## Clause 2.2 — Human Sign-off
An STR is never filed automatically. A named compliance officer must review and approve every filing.

## Clause 3.1 — KYC Status Dependency
If the customer's KYC status is PENDING or EXPIRED, the case is escalated as a control deficiency in addition to the suspicious activity finding.

## Clause 3.2 — Risk-Segment Escalation
For customers in the HIGH risk segment, internal escalation deadlines are halved (24 hours becomes 12 hours).

## Clause 4.1 — Control Deficiency Reporting
Control deficiencies, such as expired KYC on an account with flagged activity, must be reported to the Head of Compliance in the same escalation.
