# POL-LIQ-001 — Basel III Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) Compliance
Version 1.0 | Owner: Treasury & Risk | Synthetic policy for the Vigil demo.

## Clause 1.1 — Purpose
This policy sets the bank's minimum liquidity standard under Basel III.

## Clause 2.1 — LCR Requirement
LCR = High Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) ÷ Net Cash Outflows over the next 30 days. The bank must maintain an LCR of 100% or more at all times.

## Clause 3.1 — Breach Classification
Any daily snapshot with LCR below 100% is classified as a BREACH.

## Clause 3.2 — ALCO Escalation
A breach must be escalated to the Asset-Liability Committee (ALCO) within 4 hours of detection.

## Clause 4.1 — Remediation Documentation
The bank must document the cause of the breach, the remediation actions and the expected date of return to compliance.

## Clause 4.2 — Evidentiary Standard
The finding must cite the snapshot date, the HQLA amount, the net cash outflows and the computed LCR.
