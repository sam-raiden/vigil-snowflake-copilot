3. Click "Is account ACC-0031 connected to anything else?" Point at the graph, "0 of 5 individually flagged", POL-AML-001 Clause 4.5.

5. Click the liquidity question: compliant today, 94.00% breach on 2026-09-17, POL-LIQ-001 clauses.
6. Click the NPA question: LN-0025, 120 days, 15% provision; note the stated Substandard assumption.
7. Type "Why was this transaction flagged for structuring?" with no ID: the app asks which one.
